# Submission Note

Accord uses server-side exact quotation verification as its primary trust boundary. It collapses whitespace while retaining a map to original source positions, matches against the specific cited contract, and returns every exact occurrence. Model-supplied pages and offsets are never accepted. Citation placeholders remain unverified during streaming; final paragraphs with invalid citations are removed. An accepted quotation must also be covered by retrieved source ranges. Repeated and cross-page quotations are tested. Exact existence is not semantic entailment: a model can still interpret a genuine passage incorrectly, and extraction changes such as hyphenation or ligatures may cause conservative rejection.

Large documents are extracted page/paragraph-wise by a durable background worker and indexed as overlapping, word-boundary SQLite FTS5 chunks. Retrieval is bounded and performed per selected document; coverage makes the examined subset explicit. Image-only pages are not silently treated as searched. A generated 150-page PDF passed real upload/indexing tests, and final-page retrieval is separately tested. This does not certify every complex or scanned 150-page contract.
